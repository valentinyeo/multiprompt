`src/fonts/IBMPlexSans-*.ttf` contains IBM Plex Sans Regular, Italic, SemiBold, Bold, and Bold Italic, copyright IBM Corp., distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete Plex license is in [src/fonts/OFL.txt](src/fonts/OFL.txt).

`src/fonts/NordwandMono-*.ttf` contains Nordwand Mono Regular, Bold, and SemiBold by tywr, distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The bundled SemiBold face is renamed to **Nordwand Mono MP SemiBold** and modified with terminal box-drawing, block-element, and Powerline glyphs added by Multiprompt under the same SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its source is the [official 1.3.1 release](https://github.com/tywr/Nordwand-Mono/releases/tag/1.3.1). Its 24-character legacy family and full name (name IDs 1 and 4) are identical so GDI/conhost can request the distinct face at weight 600 within the 31-character FaceName limit without matching an installed upstream copy. The typographic family (ID 16) is Nordwand Mono MP with SemiBold style (ID 17); the legacy style (ID 2) remains Regular, following the upstream weight-specific GDI family convention. Its PostScript name (ID 6) is NordwandMonoMP-SemiBold. Its OS/2 code page ranges declare Latin 1, OEM 437 and OEM 850 (the 1.3.1 release declares none), so GDI selects it for the ANSI and OEM character sets conhost requests. Regenerate it with [tools/fonts/add-terminal-glyphs.py](tools/fonts/add-terminal-glyphs.py) using the original SemiBold TTF and fonttools 4.67.0. The complete license is in [src/fonts/LICENSE-NordwandMono.txt](src/fonts/LICENSE-NordwandMono.txt).
